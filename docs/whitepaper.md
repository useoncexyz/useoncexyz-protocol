# Once: Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Most market screens ask for attention all day. Once asks for a single look. The vision is a board so plain that reading it takes seconds, and so honest that nothing on it is decoration. The mission is to put the most boosted Solana tokens, and our own line, on one page that refreshes itself and stays out of the way.

## Roadmap

1. **Window.** A live board that needs no wallet and no sign-in.
2. **Line.** Our token pinned first, read from two independent feeds.
3. **Depth.** Richer per-token reads once the first two phases hold steady.
4. **Handover.** The method published so anyone can run a board of their own.
